AeroQMS is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Cheddar AI (Pty) Ltd collects, uses, stores and protects personal information in accordance with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). By using the AeroQMS platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

01 Who We Are

AeroQMS is owned and operated by Cheddar AI (Pty) Ltd, a company incorporated in the Republic of South Africa. We are the Responsible Party as defined under POPIA in respect of personal information collected through the AeroQMS platform at aeroqmsuas.co.za and app.aeroqmsuas.co.za.

For all privacy-related queries, requests or complaints, please contact us at support@aeroqmsuas.co.za.

02 Information We Collect

We collect personal information in the following categories:

Category Examples Source
Account Information Full name, email address, role, job title, organisation name Provided by you on registration
Personnel Records Pilot names, RPC licence numbers, medical certificate expiry dates Entered by organisation administrators
Operational Data Occurrence reports, hazard reports, audit records, corrective actions, investigation records Entered by users during platform use

03 How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate and maintain the AeroQMS platform and its features
  • To authenticate users and manage access to the platform
  • To send transactional notifications — audit scheduling, CAR assignments, safety notice reminders, expiry warnings
  • To generate compliance reports, dashboards and PDF exports for your organisation
  • To respond to support requests and customer service enquiries
  • To monitor platform performance, security and integrity
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations
  • To send important product updates and service announcements

We do not use your personal information for marketing purposes without your explicit consent. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes.

04 Legal Basis for Processing

Under POPIA, we process personal information on the following lawful grounds:

  • Contractual necessity — processing required to deliver the services you have subscribed to
  • Legitimate interests — platform security, fraud prevention and service improvement
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to process information to comply with applicable South African law
  • Consent — where you have expressly consented to specific processing, such as receiving marketing communications

05 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — trusted third-party providers who assist us in operating the platform, including cloud hosting, database services and transactional email delivery. These providers are contractually bound to process data only on our instruction and in accordance with POPIA
  • Legal requirements — where we are required by law, court order or regulatory authority to disclose personal information
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred to the successor entity subject to equivalent privacy protections
  • With your consent — where you have expressly authorised a specific disclosure

Our key third-party service providers currently include:

  • Supabase — database and authentication infrastructure (hosted in EU)
  • Vercel — platform hosting and deployment
  • Resend — transactional email delivery

06 Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure provided by Supabase, with servers located in the European Union (Ireland). We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/SSL
  • Encryption of data at rest
  • Role-based access controls — users only access data relevant to their role
  • Regular security monitoring and access logging
  • Secure authentication with password hashing

While we implement robust security measures, no system is completely immune to security risks. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for all activity that occurs under your account.

07 Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including:

  • Active subscriptions — all data retained for the full duration of your subscription
  • Post-termination — data retained for 30 days following subscription termination to allow for data export requests
  • Legal obligations — certain records may be retained longer where required by applicable South African law
  • Support records — communications retained for up to 2 years

After the applicable retention period, personal information is securely and permanently deleted from our systems.

08 Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Right to deletion — request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention obligations
  • Right to object — object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a written request to support@aeroqmsuas.co.za. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

If you are dissatisfied with our handling of your personal information, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforeg.org.za.

09 Cookies and Tracking

The AeroQMS platform uses the following types of cookies and local storage:

  • Essential cookies — required for authentication and session management. The platform cannot function without these.
  • Performance cookies — used to monitor platform performance and identify errors. No personally identifiable information is included.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or any third-party marketing trackers on the AeroQMS platform. The landing page at aeroqmsuas.co.za uses only essential session cookies.

10 Third-Party Services and Links

The AeroQMS platform may contain links to third-party websites or reference external services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party website or service. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you access.

Third-party service providers who process data on our behalf are bound by data processing agreements that require them to process personal information only on our instruction and in compliance with applicable data protection law.

11 Children's Privacy

The AeroQMS platform is intended for use by adults in a professional aviation context. We do not knowingly collect personal information from persons under the age of 18. If you believe that a minor's personal information has been submitted to the platform without appropriate authorisation, please contact us immediately at support@aeroqmsuas.co.za and we will take prompt action to remove such information.

12 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will notify registered users by email and update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Your continued use of the platform following notification of changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
