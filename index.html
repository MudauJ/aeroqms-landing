The Problem

Your compliance house is built on sand

Every SACAA-approved operator knows the requirements. The problem is the tools — Word documents, shared drives and email threads were never built for aviation compliance.

  • ✕
    Occurrences reported across multiple platforms — no single source of truth, no guaranteed record
  • ✕
    Incidents closed without investigation — ICAO Annex 13 requirements quietly ignored
  • ✕
    Corrective actions raised, then lost — no accountability, no follow-through, no closure
  • ✕
    Audit findings repeat year after year — because root causes were never properly identified
  • ✕
    Safety notices issued by email — no way to prove who read what and when
  • ✕
    Pilot licences and aircraft LoAs expire without warning — discovered too late
  • ✕

User Roles

The right access for every person

Four purpose-built interfaces. Each person sees only what's relevant to their role — nothing more, nothing less.

🛡️
Owner
Quality Manager / Safety Manager
  • Full platform access — all modules
  • Plan and run internal audits and inspections
  • Raise and verify corrective actions (CAPA)
  • Issue safety notices with file attachments
  • Manage user accounts and settings
  • SACAA Readiness Dashboard and escalations
  • Export all records to PDF
👔
Post Holder
AM · RP Aircraft · RP Flight Ops · Security Manager
  • Full read access to all operational data
  • Receive audit scheduling notifications
  • Complete Section B of assigned CARs
  • View completed audit reports (read-only)
  • View fleet, pilot records and expiry warnings
  • Cannot plan audits or raise CARs
🧑‍✈️
Pilot
Remote Pilot (RPC Holder)
  • Submit occurrence and hazard reports
  • Submit risk assessments for approval
  • View and acknowledge safety notices
  • Download safety notice attachments
  • Submit anonymous reports (no identity stored)
  • View own RPC and medical certificate expiry
