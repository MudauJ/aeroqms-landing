02 Description of Services

AeroQMS is a cloud-based Safety Management System (SMS) and Quality Management System (QMS) platform designed specifically for South African Unmanned Aircraft System (UAS) operators regulated by the South African Civil Aviation Authority (SACAA) under Civil Aviation Regulation 101 (CAR 101) and SA-CATS Part 101.

The Platform provides tools including but not limited to:

  • Occurrence reporting and SACAA notification management
  • ICAO-aligned accident and incident investigation workflows
  • Hazard identification and risk assessment management
  • Internal audit scheduling, checklists and findings management
  • Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) management
  • Safety notice issuance and acknowledgement tracking
  • Anonymous safety reporting channels
  • Document version control and management
  • UAS fleet and pilot licence expiry monitoring
  • SACAA compliance readiness dashboards and reporting

The Platform is provided on a subscription basis. Features available to you depend on your active subscription tier. We reserve the right to modify, add or remove features at any time with reasonable notice to subscribers.
